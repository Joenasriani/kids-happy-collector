Happy Collector

Play the game · Itch.io · Source repository

What it is

Happy Collector is a 20-level Three.js browser platformer developed as part of a multi-game interactive children's edutainment activation in the UAE.

Game structure

move and jump → collect positive blocks → avoid or stomp negative blocks → navigate obstacles → reach the glowing door → advance

The player controls a smiling yellow cube across floating platform routes. Yellow blocks carry positive emotions and qualities such as Joy, Bravery, Hope and Kindness. Red enemies carry negative emotions and behaviors such as Anger, Jealousy, Hate and Rudeness.

Collecting a yellow block awards 10 points. Stomping a red enemy from above awards 5 points. Collecting every yellow block is not required to finish a level; reaching the glowing door advances the game.

Platform challenges

The runtime includes horizontal moving platforms, vertical lifts, pendulum platforms, curved and rope-bridge sequences, button-controlled gates, button-raised steps, cracking glass platforms and enemy patrol sections.

Levels

The implemented game contains 20 levels. Levels are assembled through a deterministic blueprint system, with explicitly authored high-level sequences for Levels 11–20. Level 20 is the final level.

Controls

Desktop supports A/D or arrow keys for movement and W, Space or Up Arrow for jumping. Mobile uses on-screen movement and jump controls. Mouse-wheel and two-finger pinch zoom are available during gameplay after movement begins.

Activation context

This game belongs to the same kids-* game set developed for the multi-game interactive children's edutainment activation in the UAE.
